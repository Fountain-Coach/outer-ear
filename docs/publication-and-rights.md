# Publication and rights

The repository is **PUBLIC**. Published documents are unapproved proposal materials unless explicitly stated otherwise. Neither a named collaborating artist nor an uploaded CV establishes contractual or publication consent.

**Included now:** the two proposal/preparation PDFs supplied by the initiator, unchanged; faithful searchable transcripts; source hashes; external official grant links.

**Published artist Vita:** On 19 October 2026 Benedikt Eickhoff expressly confirmed that Karl Heinz Jeron permitted publication of his Vita and already publishes his Vita on his own site. The unchanged original and text transcript, including professional contact fields, are published based on this reported permission. No separately signed licensing document is held here, and it does not extend to other private documents.

**Agreements:** none signed or asserted here. Public or private status is undecided. Keep personal agreements, private correspondence, budgets, banking, signatures and application portal exports outside this public repository unless rights/privacy scrutiny explicitly authorizes a sanitized public version.

**License:** no automatic open license; copyright and contractual rights remain with their owners. Public repository access is not a blanket grant of reuse.

Governing policy: https://github.com/Fountain-Coach/.github/blob/main/docs/FCIS-Publication-and-Source-Policy.md
